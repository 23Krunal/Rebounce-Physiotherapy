HOME PHYSIOTHERAPY FAQ

Before your home visit.

What conditions can I ask about?

Ask about fracture recovery, orthopaedic or neurological rehabilitation, knee concerns, back pain and post-operative recovery. The physiotherapist can discuss whether a home visit is appropriate for your needs.

Which Ahmedabad areas are covered?

Home visits are offered in Gota, Ghatlodia, Drive-In Road, Thaltej and nearby areas, subject to availability. Confirm your exact address before booking.

How long is a home physiotherapy session?

Session length is confirmed when you enquire, before you accept an appointment.

How much does a home visit cost?

Ask the clinic for the current fee for your area and appointment type. The fee is confirmed before you book.

How do I request a visit?

Use the WhatsApp link or call the clinic with your area, concern and preferred time. The team will confirm availability.
