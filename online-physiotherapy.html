ONLINE PHYSIOTHERAPY FAQ

What to know beforehand.

What concerns can I ask about online?

You can ask about fracture recovery, orthopaedic and neurological concerns, knee issues, back pain and post-operative care. The physiotherapist will discuss whether online guidance suits your situation.

Do online consultations require an Ahmedabad address?

No home visit is involved in an online consultation. Contact the clinic to confirm availability for your location and preferred time.

How long is an online session?

Session length is confirmed when you enquire, before you accept an appointment.

What is the fee for an online consultation?

Ask the clinic for the current fee. It is confirmed before you book.

What happens during a video consultation?

You discuss your movement goals and concerns, and may receive exercise guidance and rehabilitation planning based on the consultation.

Can every concern be handled online?

Not necessarily. The physiotherapist will discuss whether your concern is suitable for online guidance and whether an in-person assessment may be more appropriate.
